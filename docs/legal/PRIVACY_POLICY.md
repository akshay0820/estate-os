This Privacy Policy explains how **[BLAZEREACH LEGAL ENTITY NAME]** ("**BlazeReach**", "**we**") handles personal
information in connection with Estate OS (the "**Service**").

## 1. Two roles

- **For our customers' data — we are a service provider (processor).** Real-estate businesses ("**Customers**") use the
  Service to manage their leads, clients and listings. The Customer decides what information to collect and how to use
  it, and is responsible for its own privacy notice. We process that information only on the Customer's instructions,
  under our Data Processing Agreement. **If you are a home buyer, seller, tenant or landlord and you contacted a real-estate
  business that uses Estate OS, please contact that business about your information.** We will pass any request we
  receive to the relevant Customer.
- **For our own users and business contacts — we are the business (controller).** This covers people who sign up for,
  log in to, or contact us about the Service.

## 2. Information we process

**About Customer staff who use the Service (we are the controller):**
- account details: name, work email, role and workspace membership;
- sign-in and security data: authentication records, IP address, browser type, timestamps, and audit records of actions
  such as permission changes, imports, merges and approvals;
- support communications you send us;
- billing contact details, if you pay us. **We do not store full payment card numbers.** [Payment processor, when chosen.]

**On behalf of Customers (we are the processor):** what the Customer enters or receives through the Service, which may include:
- contact details of leads and clients: name, phone, email;
- property preferences: budget, locations, bedrooms, timeline, notes;
- communication-consent records per channel and purpose, and opt-out records;
- activities, tasks, appointments and viewings;
- deals and commissions;
- listing details;
- lead-form submissions received by webhook;
- AI-generated scores, summaries and drafts.

If voice calling is enabled in the future, this also includes call records and transcripts, kept for the period the
Customer sets (default 90 days).

**We do not intentionally collect** Social Security numbers, financial account numbers, government ID numbers, health
information, or information from children under 13. The Service is for businesses and is not directed at children.

## 3. How we use information

- to provide, maintain and secure the Service, including workspace isolation, permission checks and fraud or abuse prevention;
- to run the AI features a Customer uses, on that Customer's data only;
- to provide support and send service messages: security alerts, invitations, password resets, billing notices;
- to measure usage for billing and capacity, for example AI usage metering;
- to comply with law and enforce our terms.

We do **not** sell personal information, and we do **not** share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. We do not
use Customer Data to train our own or third-party AI models.

## 4. Cookies and browser storage

The Service does not use advertising or analytics cookies or third-party tracking scripts. It stores in your browser:
- your sign-in session, so you stay logged in;
- interface preferences such as light or dark theme and sidebar state.

These are strictly necessary for the Service or remember choices you make. Web fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which
receives your IP address when the fonts load. **⚖ Counsel:** decide whether to self-host fonts to avoid this transfer.
